Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Türkçe

NodeNanny is a baby monitor that connects two or more devices in your home. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is kept and who it is shared with.

In short

  • No account, no cloud, no servers. Video and audio flow only between the devices you pair, on the same local network, encrypted end to end.
  • As the developer we do not collect, see or store any of your personal data. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software.
  • Everything the app keeps stays on your own devices and is deleted when you uninstall it.

Camera and microphone

The broadcasting device (the phone beside the baby) turns on its camera and microphone only to send live video and audio to the paired devices watching it. Motion and noise detection run on that device itself; no picture or sound is sent anywhere for them. A watching device turns on its microphone for push-to-talk only while you hold the button.

Local network

Devices find each other on the same Wi-Fi network with mDNS, announcing the name you gave the device and a randomly generated device id. A connection is set up once you confirm the six-digit code shown on both screens while pairing; all later traffic is encrypted. No outside server (STUN/TURN included) is used.

What is kept on your device

  • Settings and the device name, the list of devices you paired and the names you gave them.
  • The device identity key, used to verify pairings; its secret part is kept in the operating system's secure storage (Keychain / Keystore).
  • Broadcast history: when broadcasts started and ended, when motion and noise alerts happened, battery level and who was watching. It is shared only with the paired devices you explicitly allow.
  • Gallery: photos and videos you take while watching, in the app's own storage. They are written to your photo library only when you choose "Save to device", and shared only when you choose to.
  • Your own sound files: lullabies you add, on the broadcasting device.
  • Licence status: when the trial started, the record of the daily free watch and the package you bought.

Purchases

Packages are sold through Google Play or the App Store. Your payment details are handled only by that store; the app only sees, on your device, which package the store says you hold, and sends this to no server. The store's own privacy policy applies: Google, Apple.

Sharing

Your information is not shared with or sold to third parties. Information leaves your device only: to the devices you paired, during a live broadcast; to the devices you allowed to receive the history; or when you yourself share or export a photo, a video or a history file.

Children

The app is designed for parents and is not meant to be used by children. Your child's picture and sound go only to the devices you pair; the developer can never access them.

Deletion

You can remove a pairing in Settings › Paired devices, delete the broadcast history in Settings › Broadcast data, and delete gallery items one by one. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data on that device. The developer holds no copy to delete.

Permissions

  • Camera and microphone: for live video, audio and push-to-talk.
  • Local network and multicast: to find devices on the same Wi-Fi.
  • Adding to the photo library: only when you save a capture to the device.
  • Bluetooth and audio settings: to route sound to the speaker or a headset.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page with a new effective date.
