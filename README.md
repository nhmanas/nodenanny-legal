# NodeNanny — legal pages

The privacy policy and terms of use of the NodeNanny app, published with
GitHub Pages at https://nhmanas.github.io/nodenanny-legal/.

The app opens `tr/` or `en/` by its language (`AppUrls` in the app's
`lib/core/constants/app_urls.dart`); the privacy URL is the one given to
Google Play and App Store Connect. Keep the paths stable.
